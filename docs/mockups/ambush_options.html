Ambush: three ways to hide in grass

All three do what you asked: creeping right next to grass counts as hiding, there's more grass across the island, and there are clusters near raptor packs and lakes. They differ in how they decide whether a big T-Rex is hidden. Try a dino in the left panel; the map shows where each option puts the grass.

Heights were measured in Play on 2026-10-27: adult T-Rex top of head 20.0 studs standing, 14.0 creeping; adult raptor 5.5 standing, 3.3 creeping. Each growth stage scales them (Hatchling 0.4, Juvenile 0.6, Sub-adult 0.8, Adult 1.0). Tall grass today has blades 17–23 studs tall, so it counts as 17. New short grass would be 10 studs. Raptors and hatchlings fit under any grass in every option. The grass positions on the map are a sketch; the real ones are placed by the install script on dry, flat ground away from trees and the spawn.
