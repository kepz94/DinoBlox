Goal pool (same for every option)

StageGoalWhoCountsSize

XP reward = share of the XP your current level needs: small 35%, medium 60%, big 100% (a big goal is a whole level). Example: a hatchling at Lv 2 needs 20 XP, so a medium goal pays 12; an adult at Lv 17 needs 280, so a big goal pays 280. Every goal is counted from signals the game already sends (kills, food, water, bites, hiding, roars), so no fight or survival script changes. Rewards go through the growth bar like any other XP.
