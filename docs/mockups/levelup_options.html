Level-up: make it impossible to miss

Today a level-up is only a small purple line of text near the top for 1.8 s, with no sound and nothing on the dino. Here are three ways to make it land. Press Level up or Stage up under the screen to see each one. Stage-ups (levels 6, 11, 16) get the bigger version.
